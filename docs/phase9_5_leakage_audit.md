# Leakage audit

The audit checked 1,204 historical source snapshots and available vectors,
specifications and upstream revisions. It compares normalized exact and
alpha shapes, structural near matches and repeated metadata. Families and
upstream repositories are kept in single partitions.

All 39 records were admitted. Three generic edit-shape templates span
partitions. The audit found zero material historical or cross-partition
overlaps. The three archived Phase 9 external cases are diagnostics only.
