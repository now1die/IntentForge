# Phase 9.5 readiness

Phase 9 is available for opt-in review. The archived layout regressions are
repaired and the final cohort shows gains in bounded loop, runtime and cross-
path synthesis. The available evidence remains concentrated in the supported
grammar.

Priorities for Phase 9.5 are to expand independently documented defects across
repositories and obtain external adjudication, then explore safe summaries for
early loop exits, helper-mediated loops, and data-dependent progress. Existing
unsupported cases include nested Euclidean iteration, buffer indexing,
recursive execution limits and transfers with more than two carried locals.
Conservative abstention and source-bound manual approval should remain the
release conditions as coverage expands.
