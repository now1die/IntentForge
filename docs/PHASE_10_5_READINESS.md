# Phase 10.5 readiness

The remaining cases identify three distinct gaps. Duplicate-aware lower bounds require endpoint-valued interval contracts. Rotated-array pivot repair requires reasoning across adjacent cells, endpoint conditions and coordinated loop rewrites. Missing-member recovery needs combined range derivation and a checked XOR helper. The zero-LCM fault also motivates a bounded input-domain guard with a distinct trusted justification.

Future evaluation should freeze additional documented families from other projects and widen input domains around duplicate values, negative coordinates and empty arrays. The next increment should retain verified-output policy, raw provenance replay and restricted-worker holdout.
