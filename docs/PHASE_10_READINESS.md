# Phase 10 readiness

Phase 9.5 is ready for opt-in review. It recovered two old diagnostic defects
and one of five independent documented adaptations. The same final evaluation
shows substantial coverage of injected examples and limited coverage of
independently documented defects.

Phase 10 should broaden the documented corpus with independent adjudication
and investigate safe helper summaries for data-dependent progress and fixed
array range identities. Direct targets are the missing range scan, recursive
search bounds, the exponential interval and Euclidean helper. Finite,
source-bound evidence and manual approval remain release requirements.
