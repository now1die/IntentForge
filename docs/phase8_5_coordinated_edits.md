# Coordinated edits

Coordinated synthesis uses the Phase 8 scalar arithmetic terms. A candidate
requires at least three distinct inputs and two supplied mismatches. The search
evaluates single edits first. A fitting single suppresses the two-site stage.
For an eligible literal variant, the difference vector measures its effect
across examples. Pairs must have two nonzero, nonproportional vectors, distinct
origins and disjoint source spans. The solver then tests both edits together
against all supplied expectations.

The model considers up to eight literal sites, 96 single trials and 96 joint
trials. It explores offsets of one or two around admitted integer literals
and keeps at most four proposals. Shared compiler validation checks the complete
source once per candidate. The same proposal carries both original spans and
both replacements into one approval.

Independent coefficients in one scalar slice or a bounded pure helper can
qualify. Two-site search across branches, initialization coupled to a loop
update and three-site faults remain outside this profile.
