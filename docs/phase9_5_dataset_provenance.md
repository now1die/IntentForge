# Dataset provenance

The frozen cohort contains 39 records. Final includes 15 faults, 17 controls
and one partial specification. Development, selection and calibration each
hold one authored pair. Five final faults are adaptations of documented
upstream defects and ten are project-injected. No native production Mini-C
defects or independently human-adjudicated cases were admitted.

The specimens come from TheAlgorithms/Java and TheAlgorithms/JavaScript.
The archive retains source paths, parent and fix commits, byte-level hashes,
MIT and GPLv3 notices, and translation details. Each upstream repository is
confined to the final partition.
