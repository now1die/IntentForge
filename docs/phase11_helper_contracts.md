# Helper contracts

The model observes results from pure bounded helpers admitted by checked modulo or monotone scalar progress. A scalar loop with a self-assignment initializer is recognized through lexical identity and a checked latch even when the induction variable is declared before the loop.

A specific query supported by this phase compares a two-input helper result with unity. Discovery must vary the zero operand independently in both directions, distinguish at least one non-unity zero case, and establish agreement on two ordinary nonzero cases. The proposed guard returns a checked signed magnitude-one comparison. It avoids absolute-value overflow in the inserted syntax. The archived coprime defect is recovered behaviorally by preserving its original helper on nonzero inputs. Unmodeled side effects and incompatible typed expectations are outside the grammar.
