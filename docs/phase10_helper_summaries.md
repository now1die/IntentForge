# Pure helper summaries

The projection reuses reaching definitions and side-effect checks for local helpers. A Euclidean recurrence checks that a nonzero divisor is replaced with a remainder of strictly smaller magnitude. It respects entry into the guarded loop, signed remainder and short-circuit evaluation, and shares the transfer and iteration budgets across calls. The supported repair edits a caller's comparison when the caller and all supplied helper paths agree with the typed examples.

General data-dependent loops, cyclic effects, unsigned arithmetic and unsafe division paths are outside the admitted model. The independent zero-LCM adaptation exposes a division fault for which Phase 10 produced no matching edit.
