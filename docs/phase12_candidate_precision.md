# Candidate precision

The fresh isolated runs had zero confirmed alerts and zero speculative proposals on five controls. Two of three authored faults reached guided recommendation and both recommendations passed holdout. One authored original raised a runtime issue; its compatible candidate remained available for review separately. Neither documented fault was recommended. Confidence intervals and raw candidate counts are in the [live report](../benchmarks/reports/phase12-live-v1.json).

The current candidate policy can recommend review after a verified discovery mismatch and unique compatible alternative. This is scoped to the supplied tests. Offline holdout outcomes are reported separately. Multiple compatible repairs remain inspectable and disagreement probes carry no expected outputs. Even the sole recommendation requires manual source approval.
