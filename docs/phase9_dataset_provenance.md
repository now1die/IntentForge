# Dataset provenance

The frozen corpus contains 44 records in family-separated partitions.
Discovery, selection and calibration each have one pair. Final has 18 faults,
18 correct controls and two incomplete-specification cases. Among the 18
faults, three preserve independently documented upstream defects and 15 are
project-injected.

The upstream defects use MIT-licensed files from TheAlgorithms/Python. The
archive records upstream parents, fixes, licenses, source paths, byte-level
hashes and the exact supported adaptation. It reports zero native Mini-C
field defects and zero independent human adjudicators. Mathematical oracles
and separately compiled reference programs supply executable cross-checks.
