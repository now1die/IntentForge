# Candidate precision

Four of eight original faults produced a confirmed test mismatch. Three produced a runtime failure and one exhausted the step limit. Confirmed alert precision was 4/4 and recall was 4/8. Among the thirteen correct controls there were zero confirmed alerts, zero speculative candidates and zero visible suggestions. All six final candidates compiled and passed discovery.

The worker recommended a candidate in 3/21 cases and abstained from recommendation in 18/21. No sampled outcome had multiple discovery-passing candidates. All three recommendations passed the independent holdout. Static and behavioral top-1 and top-3 exact recovery each measured 5/8 on the identical pool.
