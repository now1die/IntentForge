# Candidate precision

The static engine exposes attempted and compatible hypotheses separately from compiler and worker results. The live profile keeps the Phase 7 verified-original policy. A correct original makes inherited alternatives optional and initially collapsed. Users can expand the same candidate IDs and inspect their results and source diffs. This presentation leaves the generation and ranking pools intact.

On seven archived Phase 7.5 controls with suggestions, the identical 17 candidates remained available and initially visible suggestions fell from 17 to zero. The new arithmetic cohort had zero control hypotheses under both Phase 7.5 and Phase 8. The archived comparison supplies the direct presentation effect. On the new cohort, confirmed alerts were 22/23 faults and 0/23 controls. There were 17 recommendations and 35 cases without one.
