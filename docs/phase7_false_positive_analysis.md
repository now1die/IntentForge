# False-positive analysis

The archived guided alerts arose from two valid unusual recurrences, an intentionally returned intermediate, and an intentional branch-specific result. Phase 6.5 conflated the existence of an alternative with fault suspicion in its static analysis. Phase 7 replays all four controls through the restricted worker. All four originals matched their supplied tests, and none received a confirmed fault. The hypotheses remained available.

On the new final cohort, Phase 6.5 guided generation alerted on 2/19 controls. The full engine 7 generator proposed hypotheses on 3/19 controls before verification. Its verified policy produced 0/19 confirmed control alerts.
