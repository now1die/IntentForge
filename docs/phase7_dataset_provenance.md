# Dataset provenance

The frozen corpus contains 60 rows including development, selection, calibration and final families. Six Mini-C ports adapt MIT-licensed TheAlgorithms/Python material at `30507bbe41397065728167fb09aa820f9fce8dc7`. Upstream sources and the license are pinned by SHA-256. The rest of the examples are project-authored. Every labeled fault is project-injected and the archive records the intended reference.

Independent behavior references use arithmetic definitions, a Josephus circle-elimination simulation, decimal string suffixes, and other executable references separate from the Mini-C edits. The verifier checks admitted correct controls, reference solutions and supplied sparse observations. Adjudication is internal. External human review was unavailable, and the final cohort fell short of the 30-fault, 30-control and 10-ambiguous research targets.
