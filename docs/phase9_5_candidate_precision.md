# Candidate precision

Phase 9.5 confirmed 13/15 faulty originals with completed output mismatches.
The remaining two returned runtime bounds issues. No confirmed alerts or
speculative proposals appeared on the 17 final controls. Optional suggestions
for matching originals continue to use the inherited collapsed presentation.

All 12 compiled candidates passed discovery and separate holdout. Three were
behaviorally accepted variants beyond the nine exact reference matches.
Three faulty cases had multiple compatible edits. Six of 33 cases received a
recommendation and all six recommendations passed holdout. Twenty-seven cases
had no recommendation.

Exact top-1 and top-3 were 8/15 and 9/15 on the same pool in both static and
behavior-guided order. Holdout agreement is scoped to the provided vectors.
