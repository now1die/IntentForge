# Leakage audit

The audit compared source and reference code against 1,058 historical sources through Phase 7.5. It applies exact and alpha-normalized keys, thresholds of 0.94 against history and 0.90 across partitions, and full specification, discovery and holdout vector comparisons. Family members stay in one partition. Shared repair signatures are reported.

Five families were quarantined, including pronic enumeration and the natural-language greater-measurement fixture. No full historical test list or specification matched under the audit normalization. Two edit signatures were shared across admitted partitions. The final set admits 52 cases, with 23 faults, 23 controls and six partial specifications. All three smaller partitions retain two cases.
