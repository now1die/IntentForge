# Phase 11 readiness

The independent interpolation case needs expressed equal-endpoint relationships and a coordinated probe arithmetic change. The coprime case requires a different denominator or helper contract than Phase 10.5's single zero operation. Rotation should next model duplicate-sensitive cyclic ordering and logical extents. The shifted missing member needs bounded coordination between a reaching extrema scan and the aggregate's endpoints, seed and index.

The duplicate-aware endpoint rules also lose proposals under two observed unused-shadow rewrites. Improving binding-equivalent recognition and validating it under a newly frozen multi-repository cohort would address that measured limitation.
